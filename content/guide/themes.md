# Themes

Open **Settings** with **Ctrl+,** or **☰ → Settings**, then **Visual**. Changes apply at once.

## For all worlds or for one

The switch at the top of Settings chooses where a change goes:

- **All worlds**: the value every world uses unless it has its own.
- **This world**: the current world only. Use it to give each game its own look, say green for one and gold for another.

A setting you have changed at the level you are editing has a small dot next to its name. Click the dot to go back to the inherited value. **This world** is greyed out when no world is open, for example on the Home screen, where **▦ Global settings** opens Settings on **All worlds**.

## Colour theme

**Colour theme** has five built-in themes:

| Theme | |
|---|---|
| **Haemal - Blood** | the default, red on near-black |
| **Amber - Phosphor** | an amber monitor |
| **Abyssal - Deep Blue** | blue |
| **Sanctum - Gold** | gold |
| **Matrix - Green** | a green monitor |

The theme colours the whole client: the menus, the panels, the Terminal. A log saved as HTML takes the colours of the theme you have when you save it.

## Your own colours

Choose **Custom - your colours**. Seven colour pickers appear, filled from the theme you had: **Background**, **Panel**, **Text**, **Accent**, **Accent bright**, **Gold** and **Alert**. μClient works out the dimmed text and the borders from them.

## Font

- **Output font**: IBM Plex Mono (the default), JetBrains Mono, Fira Code, Space Mono, Share Tech Mono, VT323 - Retro, or System Mono (the monospace font of your system).
- **Font size**: 10 to 24 px, 15 by default.
- **Line height**: 1.2 to 2, 1.5 by default.

The line under the font shows a preview. Size and line height set the Terminal and the log reader. To make one panel larger or smaller, use its own **Font** slider (right-click its tab, see [Panels and layouts](/guide/panels#font-and-opacity-per-panel)).

## Screen effects

**Settings → Effects** has the CRT look: **Scanlines** and **Scanline opacity**, **Screen flicker**, **Vignette** and **Vignette intensity**, **Phosphor glow**, and **Ember particles** with a **Particle theme** and **Ember intensity**. Turn off any you find distracting.

**Reduce motion** in **Settings → Access** stops the flicker and the embers everywhere. Until you set it, it follows your system's reduced-motion setting.

## Which of these sync

The colour theme, custom colours, font, font size and line height follow your account. The screen effects and **Reduce motion** stay on each device. See [what syncs](/guide/account#what-syncs).
