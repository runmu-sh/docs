# What μClient is

μClient connects you to MUDs, MUSHes, MOOs and other telnet games. You play in the web client at [play.runmu.sh](https://play.runmu.sh) or in the desktop app, with one account for both.

Your connections run in μClient's backend. Close the browser tab and your sessions stay connected. Sign in on another device and you find the same worlds and the same sessions. Your aliases and triggers run in the backend too, so they keep working while no device is open.

Your password and your keys stay on your device, and the server stores your data encrypted. See [Sign-in and devices](/guide/account).
